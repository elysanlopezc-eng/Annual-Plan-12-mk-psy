Section I

Course Description & Philosophical Vision

🧠

Purpose and Overview of Psychology

The Psychology course invites students to discover the principles of psychology as a science while fostering a sense of personal leadership. By exploring historical, chronological, and contextual perspectives, students learn how psychology applies to their individual lives, empowering them to understand their emotions, values, and purpose.

This course is designed to help students "Be Proactive" by developing self-awareness and emotional intelligence, enabling them to "Begin with the End in Mind" by setting meaningful goals for personal growth and personal leadership.

Section II

General Objectives

🎯
1

Psychology as an Evolving Science

Understand psychology as a historical and evolving science that improves lives by empowering individuals to take charge of their mental and emotional well-being.

2

Scientific Method Application

Apply scientific methods used in psychology to everyday challenges, reflecting the habit of "Put First Things First" in decision-making.

3

Personality & Understanding

Explore personality from various psychological and philosophical perspectives to better "Seek First to Understand" others and themselves.

4

Empathy & Professionalism

Learn to recognize and address personality traits and mental health conditions with empathy, ethical responsibility, and professionalism.

5

Holistic Well-being & Emotional Intelligence

Promote mental health and develop emotional intelligence as practical tools to "Sharpen the Saw", ensuring holistic personal and professional well-being in marketing fields.

Section IV

Achievement Indicators

📊

Reflect on Psychology's Foundations

  • Identify areas of application in psychology and its direct impact on personal life and leadership.
  • Recognize mental processes that enhance proactive behaviors and personal management.

Analyze Psychological Schools of Thought

  • Relate historical and modern theories to personal and societal contexts, reinforcing self-awareness.
  • Apply learned psychological theories in research investigations and projects with a proactive mindset.

Understand Personality and Relationships

  • Describe emotional traits and personality components that enhance interpersonal synergy.
  • Demonstrate active understanding through collaborative activities, team projects, and personal growth reflections.

Promote Mental Health and Responsible Behaviors

  • Utilize emotional intelligence for positive relationships, demonstrating a "Think Win-Win" approach.
  • Identify and discuss the ethical, cultural, and emotional aspects of life decisions and consumer behavior.
  • Cultivate personal values for future professional roles in marketing, family, and community leadership.
Section V

Institutional Didactic Methods

🧩
Active-Collaborative Pedagogical Model

The Double Period Methodology (70 Minutes)

Institutional teaching methodology designed to maximize active learning, student engagement, and practical application.

1

Motivating Introduction

Activation of prior knowledge through gamification, dynamic energizers, interactive icebreakers, and structured brainstorming sessions.

2

Individual Work

Development of individual potential, analytical skills, personal reflection, and tailored cognitive capabilities.

3

Teamwork

Structured collaborative interaction with defined student roles under the institutional motto "All for one and one for all".

4

Plenary & Reflection

Public presentation, Socratic dialogue, group debates, and synthesis based on prior home reading assignments.

Section VII

Course Contents & Thematic Sequence

🗓️

Unit I: Introduction to Psychology

Unit 1
  • • Definition and purpose of psychology as a science.
  • • Fields and historical antecedents of psychology.
  • • Main psychological models and modern perspectives.
  • • Understanding human behavior and mental processes.

Unit II: Research in Psychology

Unit 2
  • • Scientific method applied to psychological inquiry.
  • • Experimental and descriptive investigation models.
  • • Tools, questionnaires, and observation instruments for research.
  • • Ethical considerations in psychological research and consumer studies.

Unit III: Workplace Dynamics

Unit 3
  • • Emotional intelligence in professional and organizational settings.
  • • Fundamentals of industrial and organizational psychology.
  • • Evaluating job performance and talent development.
  • • Training strategies, motivation, and conflict resolution techniques.
  • • Principles of business ethics and professional leadership.
